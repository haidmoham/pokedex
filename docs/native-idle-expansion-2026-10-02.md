The native loop seam is at most 0.0027% of body extent across this batch. The source and derivative identities, pose hashes, camera distances and per-species credits are in `content/models/idle-expansion-preview-2026-10-02.json`. These are bounded CPU checks; real WebGL playback and physical-device performance remain unverified.
