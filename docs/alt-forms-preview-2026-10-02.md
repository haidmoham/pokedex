# Alternate-form release and research record

The owner-approved release adds a Forms entry on the existing species page for 120 named forms across 111 species: 62 Mega and 58 regional forms. A form is selected deliberately in Details; the normal 1,025-species feed and its default model remain unchanged. The selected form's own native HOME idle or named source battle wait plays in the existing 3D controls. Assets load on demand from an immutable upstream revision and are checked against exact byte size, Git blob SHA-1 and SHA-256 before allocation.

The source catalog is [`alt-form-preview-2026-10-02.json`](../content/models/alt-form-preview-2026-10-02.json). It records form identity, model bytes, source geometry or uploader, original wait where present, credits and unresolved rights. The already-pinned Atlas source has 106 HOME reconstructions and 14 credited external Mega models. Another 35 named Mega forms remain unavailable with verified animated sources in the inspected revisions. Source-derived geometry variants beyond this named Mega/regional set are not counted as supported forms. No form binaries are committed to Git.

All 120 normal form GLBs were locally checked against pinned byte and hash identities. All pass decoded structure, 32 MiB texture and native animation checks; 106 HOME forms also pass Three GLTFLoader and effect preflight. Three exact form files exceed the ordinary 750 KB transfer limit by at most 116 KB and have hash-bound, on-demand exceptions under 2 MB. Browser WebGL appearance, phone performance and full visual review remain open. The release count records owner-approved source-backed selections, not complete visual certification.

For Mega forms, the short Stone pulse is an authored referential transition using the [official Pokémon Mega Stone emblem](https://mega.pokemon.com/en-us). It is skipped when reduced motion is requested and can be skipped or cancelled. The form's subsequent wait is native to its source; no original-game transformation animation was found or claimed. Extracted model redistribution rights remain unresolved, regardless of an uploader's source license. The exact `2026-10-02-120` build flag gates public inclusion; unverified previews and unmapped forms remain outside it.
